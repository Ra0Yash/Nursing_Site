Patient-Centered Care

Our Healthcare Services

Professional home nursing, doctor consultations, elderly care, and patient attendants in Bhiwadi & Dharuhera.

Important Notice: Services are provided based on individual patient requirements, professional staff availability, and applicable medical guidance. Information on this page is for general service enquiries and does not constitute medical advice or emergency response.

Home Nursing Care

Our trained nurses perform essential clinical procedures at home, eliminating the need for frequent hospital visits. Procedures include intravenous (IV) infusions, intramuscular (IM) injections, urinary catheter insertion and management, Ryle's tube feeding, and Nebulization support.

Suitable For: Patients recovering from acute illness, bedridden individuals needing routine clinical nursing, and post-surgical patients.

Doctor on Call

We coordinate home visits by experienced general physicians to evaluate patient health status, adjust ongoing medications, review blood lab reports, and provide professional medical advice in the comfort of your living room.

Suitable For: Senior citizens, patients with reduced mobility, or families needing home medical consultation without clinic waiting times.
Enquire About Doctor Visit on WhatsApp

Elderly Care & Companion Assistance

Aging family members require constant empathy and attentive care. Our senior care service assists elders with daily activities such as walking support, medication administration, vitals recording, light exercise, and warm companionship.

Suitable For: Elderly parents living independently or requiring daily routine assistance while working family members are away.
Enquire About Elderly Care on WhatsApp

Patient Attendant Services (Bedside Assistance)

Our dedicated male and female patient attendants provide 12-hour or 24-hour shift assistance for bedridden or semi-dependent patients. Tasks include sponge bathing, oral hygiene, diaper changes, position shifting to prevent bedsores, and assistance with meals.

Suitable For: Patients recovering from stroke, bone fractures, paralytic conditions, or severe weakness needing round-the-clock physical aid.
Enquire About Patient Attendant on WhatsApp

Post-Hospitalisation Recovery Care

Transitioning from hospital discharge back to home requires careful medical supervision. Our nurses manage surgical drain care, wound inspection, prescribed oral and injectable medications, and assist in physical recovery under medical guidelines.

Suitable For: Patients recently discharged after major surgery, cardiac procedures, orthopedic replacements, or prolonged ICU stays.
Enquire About Post-Hospital Care on WhatsApp

Wound Dressing & Basic Nursing Procedures

Sterile wound dressing services performed at your home to prevent infection. We handle diabetic foot ulcers, surgical incision dressings, bed sore wound care, vacuum dressing assistance, and suture/staple removal.

Suitable For: Patients with non-healing wounds, post-op surgical cuts, or pressure sores requiring frequent aseptic dressing changes.
Enquire About Wound Dressing on WhatsApp

Health & Vital Signs Monitoring

Consistent tracking of blood pressure, blood glucose levels (glucometer checks), oxygen saturation (SPO2), respiratory rate, and pulse rate. Maintenance of daily digital or paper vital logs for physician review.

Suitable For: Diabetic, hypertensive, COPD, or cardiac patients requiring regular vital parameters tracking.
Enquire About Health Monitoring on WhatsApp

Long-Term Patient Support

Comprehensive monthly or long-term nursing packages for chronic conditions. We assign consistent nursing staff to build familiar, comfortable relationships with patients needing long-duration care.

Suitable For: Chronic illness management, paralysis care, or long-term palliative comfort support at home.
