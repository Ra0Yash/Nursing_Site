Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

How Nursing On Call protects visitor information and handles service enquiries.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with Nursing On Call via our website (nursingoncall.in), phone calls, or WhatsApp messages, we may collect basic personal information provided voluntarily by you, including:

  • Your full name and contact phone number
  • Service location details (e.g. city, sector in Bhiwadi or Dharuhera)
  • Nature of care or nursing service requested
  • Brief details regarding patient condition necessary to assess caregiver suitability

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected is used strictly for operational purposes to:

  • Respond directly to your service queries and WhatsApp messages
  • Coordinate home nursing visits, doctor calls, or attendant placement
  • Communicate schedules and care requirements with assigned healthcare staff

3. Data Protection & Non-Disclosure

We respect family privacy. We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal phone numbers, patient details, or location information with any third-party marketing agencies or external brokers. Your information is accessed only by authorized personnel involved in delivering care.

4. WhatsApp & External Services

When you click WhatsApp buttons on our website, you enter a chat session governed by WhatsApp / Meta’s standard privacy policies. We encourage visitors to avoid sending sensitive financial or banking details over messaging channels.

5. Contacting Us Regarding Privacy
